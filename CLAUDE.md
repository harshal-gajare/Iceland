# CLAUDE.md

## What this is
Single-file interactive trip planner for a 10-night family (3 people, one 11-year-old) Ring Road + Snæfellsnes loop of Iceland, Sep 19–29 2026. One itinerary. (It carried a second, Plan B / highland edition, behind tabs until Aug 2026 — `git log` has it if it is ever wanted back.) Everything ships in `index.html` plus a thin PWA shell: no build step, no dependencies, works from disk (`file://`) and installs offline over http(s).

## Architecture
- `index.html` — all markup, CSS, vanilla JS inline.
- **`TRIP` object is the single source of truth**, carrying `desc`, `days[]`, `upgrades[]` (chip label + day number), `reservations[]` (`{when,what,ref,tel,meta}` — the car and the timed tours only) and `stats` (km/stops/soaks/flex; `stops` is computed by reduce, `flex` is raw HTML injected with innerHTML). `DAYS` is one flat array, days 1–10 in order.
- **Nothing is shared by reference any more.** Days used to be split across `DAY1` / `D5TO10` / `CLASSIC_DAYS` so the shared ones could hang off both plans, which made in-place mutation corrupting. With one itinerary `DAYS` is a plain flat array and that hazard is gone — `dayLight(d, prev)` is still a pure reader taking the predecessor as an argument, which is worth keeping.
- **Reservations are half-derived.** `buildReservations()` generates every lodging row from `DAYS[].stay` — name, address and terms exist once, on the object the day card also renders, so the two cannot disagree; unbooked nights fall out of the same pass and render dimmed. Only the car and the timed tours live in `TRIP.reservations`, because their references sit in prose inside stop notes. Don't hand-copy a stay into that array.
- **Day shape:** `{n, dow, date, km, depart, arrive, title, base, baseLL:[lat,lon], stay?|hotels[], note?, stops[]}`. A `stay` object (`{name, addr, ll, q, status}`) marks a confirmed booking and renders the green Booked card (`q: null` falls back to lat/lon map links); `hotels[]` renders budget-pick chips linking to Google Maps searches. `note` renders as the amber day-level callout (weather gates, alternatives). `food?:{lunch?,dinner?,coffee?,sweet?,fuel?,shop?,buy?,extra?}` renders the dashed practicalities box below the stops — `fuel` starts the logistics half and takes the hairline — `extra` is deliberately the pick that *doesn't* fit the run-sheet, so keep the reason it doesn't in the text. Unlike stop notes these strings carry `<b>` on purpose (innerHTML, same as `stats.flex`); the generator converts it to markdown bold rather than stripping it.
- **The departure day is `DEPARTURE`, not `DAYS[10]`.** Sep 29 renders as card **11** — same summary, same run-sheet, and `--d11`, one more step of the day spectrum past day 10's rose — but it is a **separate object and must stay out of `DAYS`**: the hero's km/stops/soaks and `#st-booked` all reduce over `DAYS`, the map has no route or base ring to draw for a day with no leg and no night, and a rail pill for it would dim the whole map to nothing. **The number and the color are presentation; membership in `DAYS` is not.** `buildDeparture()` renders it into `#depcard` and `stopList()` is shared with `buildCards()` so the two run-sheets cannot diverge. Its sun times go through `dayLight(DEPARTURE, DAYS[9])` — sunrise at Kríunes, sunset at KEF — because the block it replaced had a **hand-typed "Sunrise 07:33" that was 3 minutes off the page's own math**; don't reintroduce a literal. It carries `keys` and `flight` where a day carries `km` and `stay`.
- **Stop shape:** `{name, ll?:[lat,lon], time, drive?, tag?, note, shot?, pq?, tide?, offroute?}`. `tag` ∈ `booked | book | soak | opt`. `booked` and `book` are the same thing at different stages — both are timed reservations and both render amber on the map; the card chip distinguishes them (green "Booked" vs amber "Book now"). Flip a stop to `booked` when its confirmation lands, and put the reference in the note. `drive` renders the dashed connector ("drive 45 min") before the stop. A stop with `ll` gets Google (`gLL`) and Apple (`aLL`) map links; `ll` is **optional only for `DEPARTURE`'s logistics rows** — the fuel-and-keys handover is a time with nowhere to pin, since Blue Car's counter isn't in this file. Every stop in `DAYS` has coordinates and must keep them, or it vanishes from the map. **`offroute?`** marks a pin the hire car cannot drive to — only Katla ice cave, whose `ll` is the cave up on Mýrdalsjökull, reached in the operator's super jeep from Vík. It keeps the pin but is excluded from the KML's driving line, which otherwise routed a car onto an icecap and overshot the day by 55 km. **`shot?`** is the photograph worth taking there, rendered as the smaller ◈ line under the note by `stopList()`; 55 of 60 stops carry one and the five that don't are pure logistics (airport, hotel departure, lunch, fuel-and-keys, terminal). Like `food` and unlike `note` it **carries `<b>` on purpose**, and the generator runs it through `bold()`. Its light claims are not decoration: sun elevations and bearings were computed per stop from `solarPos()` at each stop's own start time, so "front-lit at 10:15" is checkable — **don't reword a direction without recomputing it.** The trip-wide light facts live once in the **Photographs** field note, which is why the per-stop lines can stay to a sentence or two. A stop with a `shot` also gets a third `.mlinks` chip, **◈**, opening an image search for the subject — **reference images by link, never embedded**: the photographs belong to whoever took them and this page is deployed publicly, so nothing is reproduced and nothing needs licensing. `pIMG()` derives the query from the name (first segment before a `·`, trailing `— tail` dropped) and appends " Iceland" unless the name already says it; **`pq?` overrides the query** where that rule picks the wrong subject, which it does on four stops — Svartifoss, Perlan, Gatklettur and Deildartunguhver are each the second name in a composite stop.
- **Two map views.** The stylized SVG is the default and the only one that works offline, dims to a single day from the rail, and follows the theme. A `Real map` toggle swaps in a **Google My Maps iframe** (`maps/d/embed?mid=` — no API key, no billing) built from `MYMAP_MID`; empty ships the setup note instead of a broken frame. **The iframe is inserted lazily and only when `navigator.onLine` is true, and that gate is the whole design, not caution:** `sw.js` returns without `respondWith()` for cross-origin, so a frame that cannot load renders the browser's own error document inside the box, and that is **undetectable from the page** — `onerror` does not fire for a cross-origin frame and `onload` may fire for the error page. Google's terms also forbid caching tiles, so it can never join `SHELL`. Never insert speculatively and try to catch the failure. On a failed mount the note shows *beside* the stylized map rather than replacing it, and the button keeps offering `Real map` so tapping again in signal just works. `#realmap` must never get a `display` property or `[hidden]` breaks — and `.legend` **does** set `display:flex`, which is why it needs its own `.legend[hidden]` rule.
- **Map:** static layer built once (`buildMapStatic`: hand-simplified `COAST` polygon, glacier ellipses, KEF pulse) + dynamic layer (`buildMapDynamic`: routes + stop/base markers inside `#dyn`). Equirectangular projection with cos(65°) correction (`px()`); `smoothPath()` is Catmull-Rom → cubic bezier. Stylized — not navigation-grade.
- **Map colors must stay `var(--d{n})`, never resolved hex** (`dayVar()`), so the map flips theme live without a rebuild. They must be assigned via `.style.stroke`/`.style.fill`, **never as SVG presentation attributes** — `stroke="var(--d3)"` is invalid as an attribute and silently falls back to initial (invisible routes, black markers).
- **Sync:** `render()` builds dyn map, rail, cards, upgrades, reservations and hero stats (`#st-km/#st-stops/#st-soak/#st-flex`) plus the trip description, then resets to "all days"; `selectDay(n)` syncs rail pills ↔ map dimming ↔ auto-opened `<details>` cards.
- **Persistence** (`localStorage`, all reads/writes try/catch'd so `file://` private mode still works): `iceland26.theme` and `iceland26.mapview`. A saved `real` map view is only restored when `MYMAP_MID` is set **and** `navigator.onLine` — otherwise you would come back to an explanation where you left a map. The booking tracker's per-index ticks (`iceland26.bk*`) went with the tracker when the page became a travel reference — which is what freed `reservations[]` to be reordered, since array position used to be load-bearing for restoring ticks.
- **Theme:** pre-paint `<head>` script applies the stored choice or `prefers-color-scheme`; the top-bar toggle saves only on explicit click. Design tokens live in `:root` with a full override set under `:root[data-theme="light"]`.
- **Daylight** (`DAYLIGHT` block at the end of the DATA section) — sunrise/sunset are **computed, never typed in**. NOAA solar-position equations, run **twice**: declination shifts ~23′/day near the equinox, so evaluating it once at midday skews sunrise and sunset ~2 min in *opposite* directions. Iceland is UTC+0 year-round, so UTC minutes are local minutes. Flat sea horizon (zenith 90.833° = refraction + solar radius), verified to the minute against published tables at Höfn (0 m); elevation dip is **deliberately not modelled** — it's worth 2–3 min while Iceland's real horizon is mountains worth far more, and the page says so in the section lede. A day's **sunrise is taken at the previous night's base** (where you wake and set off) and its **sunset at that day's own base**; on day 8 those are 5° of longitude apart, ~20 min, on a 450 km driving day. `sunEvent()` returns `null` for polar day/night and the strip is omitted rather than rendering dashes. Everything here is a **pure reader** — `dayLight(d, prev)` takes the predecessor as an argument precisely so nothing is memoized onto a day object.
- **Live tide strip** (`loadTide()`) on any stop carrying `tide:true` — currently only Stokksnes, where the Vestrahorn reflection needs wet sand. **Tide is fetched, not computed, and that asymmetry with daylight is deliberate:** harmonic prediction needs published constants for a specific station and this file has none, so inventing a number would be worse than omitting one. Open-Meteo's marine API (`sea_level_height_msl`) is free, keyless and answers `access-control-allow-origin:*` even to a `null` origin, so it works from `file://` like NOAA's. Iceland is UTC+0, so `timezone=UTC` returns local times directly. Samples are hourly, so each turning point is refined by fitting a parabola through it and its neighbours — the raw minimum is only good to the hour. The **16-day horizon is guarded client-side**: a date beyond it would 400 on every load, so the request is skipped rather than fired and caught. Like `.kpline`, **never give `.tideline` a `display` property** or `[hidden]` stops working.
- **Live Kp strip** in the aurora card fetches NOAA SWPC's `noaa-planetary-k-index-forecast.json` (CORS `*`, so it works from `file://` too). vedur.is sends no CORS headers so it is link-out only. On any failure the strip stays `hidden` — never give `.kpline` a `display` property, or `[hidden]` stops working and an empty strip renders on every failure.
- **`sw.js`** — navigations network-first with cached `index.html` fallback; shell assets cache-first; cross-origin (NOAA) passes through untouched. Cache name `iceland26-v5` — **bump the version when changing cached shell files.** It matters most for the cache-first assets (icons, manifest), which are otherwise never re-fetched; `index.html` also refreshes on any successful navigation, but a bump is what guarantees an offline-only client gets the new fallback copy. Registration is guarded to https/localhost, so `file://` use is unaffected. **A bump does not take effect at once on the live site:** `sw.js` is served with `cache-control: max-age=14400`, so Cloudflare's edge keeps the old file for up to four hours (`cf-cache-status: HIT`) while the origin already has the new one — confirm with `curl 'https://harshalgajare.com/Iceland/sw.js?cb=1'`, which uses a fresh cache key and shows the truth. It self-heals when the TTL expires; purge the Cloudflare cache if you need it sooner. Don't chase a bump that looks like it failed.
- **`manifest.json`** + **`icons/`** — installable PWA identity (standalone, night-sky aurora icon).
- Sections: hero (countdown to ATL 18:45 Sep 18), trip description, map, sticky day rail, day cards (each stop optionally carrying its ◈ photo line), reservations, "If something goes wrong" emergency block, "Locked in" logistics (flights, rental), aurora game plan, "The daily watch" resources, field notes, footer.
- **`docs/iceland-2026.kml`** is generated — `node tools/build-kml.mjs` (`--check` for staleness, `--route` to re-fetch road geometry). **Legs follow real roads**, not straight lines between stops: geometry comes from OSRM's public router (OpenStreetMap, ODbL — the KML carries the attribution) and is cached in **`tools/route-cache.json`**, keyed by each leg's waypoint string. That cache is committed on purpose — the default build and `--check` never touch the network, so they stay deterministic and offline, and OSM shifting under us cannot silently rewrite a committed map. Move a stop and the key moves, the cache misses, and the build **names the days that fell back to chords** instead of shipping them quietly. OSRM returns a point every few metres, so geometry is decimated with Douglas-Peucker at ~40 m — 112 KB total against My Maps' 5 MB cap. **`drivePts()` decides what bends the line, and it is not every pin:** `opt` stops are branches (routing through Fagradalsfjall sent day 10 to Reykjanes and back; Seyðisfjörður sent day 5 over a pass it may never cross) and `offroute` stops are unreachable by car. Both stay pinned. It is what the real-map view points at: per-day `<Folder>`s with a placemark per stop, a `LineString` leg and the overnight base, styled in the same `--d1..--d11` spectrum read straight out of `:root`. **My Maps has no API**, so importing it is a manual step — and the imported map is a **snapshot, not a view**: regenerating `docs/iceland-2026.kml` leaves the live map unchanged, so any edit to a stop's `name`, `note` or coordinates silently drifts out of sync until someone re-imports by hand. That bit in Aug 2026 — stripping the car's booking reference from the page left it still readable in the Day 1 "Keflavík Airport → Blue Car" placemark on the public map. **Anything sensitive has to be removed in both places.** Per-placemark editing in My Maps is usually faster than a re-import, which costs the ten-layer dance again. The map is capped at **10 layers** and an 11-folder import **silently drops the last one** — that is not a risk, it happened: the first real import lost Sep 29. So the file emits **10 folders**, with the departure day riding inside day 10's, and the generator **throws** rather than warns if it ever regenerates past ten. The live map is `MYMAP_MID` in `index.html`. Three traps the script exists to contain: KML `<coordinates>` is **`lon,lat`** where this project stores **`[lat, lon]`** (reversed in exactly one helper, `coord()`); KML colour is **`aabbggrr`**, so `#3cc9e8` is `ffe8c93c`; and `note`/`shot` carry real `<b>` and literal `&amp;`, so descriptions go in `CDATA` and everything else through an XML escape — `esc()` from the itinerary generator escapes `|` for markdown and is the wrong tool. The one `DEPARTURE` stop without `ll` is skipped.
- **`docs/itinerary.md`** is generated — run `node tools/build-itinerary.mjs` after changing the itinerary, or `--check` to fail if it's stale. **`tools/build-itinerary.mjs`** evaluates the `DATA` section of `index.html` in a `node:vm` sandbox and renders the day table, base table, **departure section** and booking checklist straight from `TRIP`/`DEPARTURE`; the remaining prose sections are transcribed in the script's own template, so page copy and doc copy have to be edited together. It also pulls `dayLight`/`hhmm`/`hoursMins`/`shortBase` out of the sandbox rather than re-deriving daylight, and throws if `dayLight` or `DEPARTURE` goes missing — so the doc's sun times cannot drift from the page's. It keys off the `/* ==== DATA ==== */` and `/* ==== MAP ==== */` comment markers — renaming those breaks it loudly, by design. It's a docs tool, not a build step: `index.html` still ships as-is.

## Conventions
- Stay single-file plus PWA shell and dependency-free unless there's a strong reason not to. **The one exception is the My Maps iframe**, and it is gated rather than trusted: nothing third-party loads until the user asks for it and the network is known to be up, so the default page still has zero external subresources.
- Design tokens in `:root`; `--d1..--d11` day spectrum is an information system (`--d11` is the departure card only — nothing on the map uses it), not decoration; `--ember` = bookable, teal = soak. All theme-sensitive colors are tokens — never reintroduce literals. Deliberate exceptions kept literal: the soak teal, chip inks, the aurora border gradient, and the low-alpha green/amber tints.
- **Legend labels describe marker rules; statline numbers are trip facts.** Amber = "timed booking" (`tag:"book"` or `"booked"`), teal = "walk-in soak" (`tag:"soak"`). The hero's anchor-tour and soak-option counts are hand-maintained trip facts, not derived — the source comment above `TRIP.stats` records the current tally and what each number counts, so update it and the number together. Some soaks are paid, timed tickets and carry `booked` (Blue Lagoon, Earth Lagoon); they count as soaks rather than tours, so they never inflate the anchor-tour number. Keep the two vocabularies disjoint — don't "reconcile" them into one count.
- **A day's `depart` is sometimes load-bearing, not taste.** Day 7 departs 07:55 because whale check-in closes 08:50 for the 09:30 sailing and the drive from Mývatn is 45 min; a missed check-in is charged in full. This time has moved every time the tours were rebooked (GG1 09:45 → GG2 09:30 → GG2 12:30 → GG2 09:30, with the Saltvík ride going 10:00 → 14:00 and 1.5 hrs → 1 hr). **The two bookings used to collide** — a 10:00 ride ending after an 11:50 check-in — and the current split, whales in the morning and horses at 14:00, is what pulled them apart; the stop order in the array follows it, so don't reshuffle day 7 without reading both stop notes. **Don't push it to 08:10 for the Dimmuborgir breakfast either** — service starts 08:00, the last departure that still makes check-in is 08:05 with zero margin, and a missed check-in is charged in full; the breakfast gets packed instead. Day 10 is the same shape: `08:10` exists because Into the Glacier starts 10:30 at Klaki, 1 hr 40 away up Road 550, and the day's stop list was cut down (Raufarhólshellir dropped, Hraunfossar demoted to an either/or) to give Fagradalsfjall real daylight — see the comment above the day. **Fagradalsfjall deliberately carries no clock in its `time`**: it is the alternative to Krauma, not a stop after it, so its `drive` is measured from Hraunfossar — the branch point — and the run-sheet must not be "fixed" to chain through it. (This branch used to point the other way, with Hraunfossar as the swap; it was inverted Aug 2026.) Don't "tidy" a departure time without checking what constrains it — the reason is in a comment above the day.
- Preserve accessibility floor: visible `:focus-visible` styles, keyboard-operable map stops (`Enter`/`Space`), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, print stylesheet working (all `<details>` auto-expand on `beforeprint`; controls, map links and the Kp strip hide in print).
- **Anything derivable from `TRIP` is derived, not typed into the markup** — `#st-km`, `#st-stops`, `#st-soak`, `#st-flex` and `#st-booked` (nights with a confirmed `stay`) are all written by `render()`. Nights 8–10 are booked now, but the count stays derived — a literal was what would have gone stale, and `buildReservations()` still has to handle an unbooked night if one is ever released. `10 nights` and the `$145–255` room-rate range are the only intentionally static numbers.
- **Cloudflare rewrites `mailto:` links** on the deployed site (Email Address Obfuscation), swapping them for a `/cdn-cgi/` path decoded by an injected script. That script is not in the service worker's `SHELL`, so an obfuscated address renders as garbage offline — which is unacceptable in the emergency block. The embassy emails are therefore wrapped in `<!--email_off-->` / `<!--email_on-->`, Cloudflare's own opt-out. Emails inside the inline `<script>` (stop notes) are untouched because the rewriter only walks HTML.
- All asset paths stay **relative** so subpath hosting works. The sole exception is the `og:url` / `og:image` social-preview pair, which scrapers require to be absolute — update those two if the deploy host moves.
- CSS: flat selectors, tokens over literals; watch specificity collisions. Two live examples: the day-dimming rule needs `#icemap .route path.dim` because a bare `#icemap .dim` loses to `#icemap .route path`; and print token overrides must target `:root[data-theme="light"]`/`[data-theme="dark"]` too, or the theme attribute out-specifies them.
- UI copy: sentence case, plain verbs, field-log tone. Mono face for anything data-like.
- `localStorage` keys are namespaced `iceland26.*`.
- Booked-stay facts, flight times, insurance list and booking references are ground truth — never alter without explicit instruction. ## Backlog
- **Day km are the routed through-route distance, measured, not estimated.** Set from OSRM in Aug 2026 and rounded to the nearest 10; `stats.km` is their exact sum (2,250) and must stay so. Re-measure with `node tools/build-kml.mjs --route`, which prints declared against routed for every leg. **What they exclude is deliberate:** `opt` stops are branches, so the number is what you drive if you do the planned stops. The three optionals that cost real distance carry it in their own notes — Petra's **+68 km** (Route 1 cuts inland to Egilsstaðir; Petra's commits you to the fjord coast, and it is the biggest detour on the trip), Seyðisfjörður **+53 km** over Fjarðarheiði, and Fagradalsfjall **+85 km**, which is measured against the Krauma day because it *replaces* Krauma rather than adding to it. The old hand-authored figures were wrong in both directions — day 8 read `~390` and routes at 450, day 1 read `~100` and routes at 69 — and day 8 was separately described as 430 km in two food notes and the daylight comment, so three numbers disagreed about the longest drive of the trip.

## Deploy
Any static host; all asset paths are relative so subpath hosting works (currently GitHub Pages → `harshalgajare.com/Iceland/`). GitHub Pages: serve repo root. Azure Static Web Apps: app location `/`, no API, no build step.
