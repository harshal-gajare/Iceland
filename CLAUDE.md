# CLAUDE.md

## What this is
Single-file interactive trip planner for a 10-night, family-friendly Ring Road + Snæfellsnes loop of Iceland, timed for Sep 19–29. It began as one family's real trip (Sep 2026) and was **made generic for sharing in Oct 2026** — see the no-personal-data rule under Conventions, which is the first thing to read before editing. One itinerary. Everything ships in `index.html` plus a thin PWA shell: no build step, no dependencies, works from disk (`file://`) and installs offline over http(s).

## Architecture
- `index.html` — all markup, CSS, vanilla JS inline.
- **`TRIP` object is the single source of truth**, carrying `desc`, `days[]`, `upgrades[]` (chip label + day number), `reservations[]` (`{when,what,tel,meta}` — the car and the timed tours worth booking ahead) and `stats` (km/stops/soaks/flex; `stops` is computed by reduce, `flex` is raw HTML injected with innerHTML). `DAYS` is one flat array, days 1–10 in order.
- **Nothing is shared by reference any more.** Days used to be split across `DAY1` / `D5TO10` / `CLASSIC_DAYS` so the shared ones could hang off both plans, which made in-place mutation corrupting. With one itinerary `DAYS` is a plain flat array and that hazard is gone.
- **The book-ahead list is half-derived.** `buildReservations()` generates every lodging row from `DAYS[].stay` — name, address and room notes exist once, on the object the day card also renders, so the two cannot disagree; a night with no stay falls out of the same pass and renders dimmed. Only the car and the timed tours live in `TRIP.reservations`. Don't hand-copy a stay into that array.
- **Day shape:** `{n, dow, date, km, depart, arrive, title, base, baseLL:[lat,lon], stay?|hotels[], note?, stops[]}`. A `stay` object (`{name, addr, ll, q, status}`) is where this route sleeps and renders the neutral stay card (`q: null` falls back to lat/lon map links); `status` holds room options, check-in hours and a public phone number — never anything that came from a booking; `hotels[]` renders budget-pick chips linking to Google Maps searches. `note` renders as the amber day-level callout (weather gates, alternatives). `food?:{lunch?,dinner?,coffee?,sweet?,fuel?,shop?,buy?,extra?}` renders the dashed practicalities box below the stops — `fuel` starts the logistics half and takes the hairline — `extra` is deliberately the pick that *doesn't* fit the run-sheet, so keep the reason it doesn't in the text. Unlike stop notes these strings carry `<b>` on purpose (innerHTML, same as `stats.flex`); the generator converts it to markdown bold rather than stripping it.
- **The departure day is `DEPARTURE`, not `DAYS[10]`.** Sep 29 renders as card **11** — same summary, same run-sheet, and `--d11`, one more step of the day spectrum past day 10's rose — but it is a **separate object and must stay out of `DAYS`**: the hero's km/stops/soaks and `#st-booked` all reduce over `DAYS`, the map has no route or base ring to draw for a day with no leg and no night, and a rail pill for it would dim the whole map to nothing. **The number and the color are presentation; membership in `DAYS` is not.** `buildDeparture()` renders it into `#depcard` and `stopList()` is shared with `buildCards()` so the two run-sheets cannot diverge. It carries `keys` and `flight` where a day carries `km` and `stay` — and `flight` is a generic "late afternoon", not anyone's flight.
- **Stop shape:** `{name, ll?:[lat,lon], time, drive?, tag?, note, shot?, pq?, offroute?}`. `tag` ∈ `book | soak | opt`. `book` is a timed reservation: amber on the map, a "Book ahead" chip on the card. (A separate `booked` state for confirmed tickets existed while this was a personal trip and went with the personal data — don't bring it back.) `drive` renders the dashed connector ("drive 45 min") before the stop. A stop with `ll` gets Google (`gLL`) and Apple (`aLL`) map links; `ll` is **optional only for `DEPARTURE`'s logistics rows** — the fuel-and-keys handover is a time with nowhere to pin, since the rental counter isn't in this file. Every stop in `DAYS` has coordinates and must keep them, or it vanishes from the map. **`offroute?`** marks a pin the hire car cannot drive to — only Katla ice cave, whose `ll` is the cave up on Mýrdalsjökull, reached in the operator's super jeep from Vík. It keeps the pin but is excluded from the KML's driving line, which otherwise routed a car onto an icecap and overshot the day by 55 km. **`shot?`** is the photograph worth taking there, rendered as the smaller ◈ line under the note by `stopList()`. Like `food` and unlike `note` it **carries `<b>` on purpose**, and the generator runs it through `bold()`. **Shot lines carry no sun angles and no sun times** — elevations, compass bearings of the sun and "front-lit at 10:15"-style clocks were stripped on request in Oct 2026, together with the daylight machinery that computed them. Keep composition advice; don't reintroduce solar geometry. A stop with a `shot` also gets a third `.mlinks` chip, **◈**, opening an image search for the subject — **reference images by link, never embedded**: the photographs belong to whoever took them and this page is deployed publicly, so nothing is reproduced and nothing needs licensing. `pIMG()` derives the query from the name (first segment before a `·`, trailing `— tail` dropped) and appends " Iceland" unless the name already says it; **`pq?` overrides the query** where that rule picks the wrong subject, which it does on four stops — Svartifoss, Perlan, Gatklettur and Deildartunguhver are each the second name in a composite stop.
- **Two map views.** The stylized SVG is the default and the only one that works offline, dims to a single day from the rail, and follows the theme. A `Real map` toggle swaps in a **Google My Maps iframe** (`maps/d/embed?mid=` — no API key, no billing) built from `MYMAP_MID`; empty ships the setup note instead of a broken frame. **The iframe is inserted lazily and only when `navigator.onLine` is true, and that gate is the whole design, not caution:** `sw.js` returns without `respondWith()` for cross-origin, so a frame that cannot load renders the browser's own error document inside the box, and that is **undetectable from the page** — `onerror` does not fire for a cross-origin frame and `onload` may fire for the error page. Google's terms also forbid caching tiles, so it can never join `SHELL`. Never insert speculatively and try to catch the failure. On a failed mount the note shows *beside* the stylized map rather than replacing it, and the button keeps offering `Real map` so tapping again in signal just works. `#realmap` must never get a `display` property or `[hidden]` breaks — and `.legend` **does** set `display:flex`, which is why it needs its own `.legend[hidden]` rule.
- **Map:** static layer built once (`buildMapStatic`: hand-simplified `COAST` polygon, glacier ellipses, KEF pulse) + dynamic layer (`buildMapDynamic`: routes + stop/base markers inside `#dyn`). Equirectangular projection with cos(65°) correction (`px()`); `smoothPath()` is Catmull-Rom → cubic bezier. Stylized — not navigation-grade.
- **Map colors must stay `var(--d{n})`, never resolved hex** (`dayVar()`), so the map flips theme live without a rebuild. They must be assigned via `.style.stroke`/`.style.fill`, **never as SVG presentation attributes** — `stroke="var(--d3)"` is invalid as an attribute and silently falls back to initial (invisible routes, black markers).
- **Sync:** `render()` builds dyn map, rail, cards, upgrades, reservations and hero stats (`#st-km/#st-stops/#st-soak/#st-flex`) plus the trip description, then resets to "all days"; `selectDay(n)` syncs rail pills ↔ map dimming ↔ auto-opened `<details>` cards.
- **Persistence** (`localStorage`, all reads/writes try/catch'd so `file://` private mode still works): `iceland26.theme` and `iceland26.mapview`. A saved `real` map view is only restored when `MYMAP_MID` is set **and** `navigator.onLine` — otherwise you would come back to an explanation where you left a map. The booking tracker's per-index ticks (`iceland26.bk*`) went with the tracker when the page became a travel reference — which is what freed `reservations[]` to be reordered, since array position used to be load-bearing for restoring ticks.
- **Theme:** pre-paint `<head>` script applies the stored choice or `prefers-color-scheme`; the top-bar toggle saves only on explicit click. Design tokens live in `:root` with a full override set under `:root[data-theme="light"]`.
- **Live Kp strip** in the aurora card fetches NOAA SWPC's `noaa-planetary-k-index-forecast.json` (CORS `*`, so it works from `file://` too). vedur.is sends no CORS headers so it is link-out only. On any failure the strip stays `hidden` — never give `.kpline` a `display` property, or `[hidden]` stops working and an empty strip renders on every failure.
- **`sw.js`** — navigations network-first with cached `index.html` fallback; shell assets cache-first; cross-origin (NOAA) passes through untouched. Cache name `iceland26-v5` — **bump the version when changing cached shell files.** It matters most for the cache-first assets (icons, manifest), which are otherwise never re-fetched; `index.html` also refreshes on any successful navigation, but a bump is what guarantees an offline-only client gets the new fallback copy. Registration is guarded to https/localhost, so `file://` use is unaffected. **A bump does not take effect at once on the live site:** `sw.js` is served with `cache-control: max-age=14400`, so Cloudflare's edge keeps the old file for up to four hours (`cf-cache-status: HIT`) while the origin already has the new one — confirm with `curl 'https://harshalgajare.com/Iceland/sw.js?cb=1'`, which uses a fresh cache key and shows the truth. It self-heals when the TTL expires; purge the Cloudflare cache if you need it sooner. Don't chase a bump that looks like it failed.
- **`manifest.json`** + **`icons/`** — installable PWA identity (standalone, night-sky aurora icon).
- Sections: hero, trip description, map, sticky day rail, day cards (each stop optionally carrying its ◈ photo line), book ahead, "If something goes wrong" emergency block, car hire, aurora game plan, "The daily watch" resources, field notes, footer.
- **`docs/iceland-2026.kml`** is generated — `node tools/build-kml.mjs` (`--check` for staleness, `--route` to re-fetch road geometry). **Legs follow real roads**, not straight lines between stops: geometry comes from OSRM's public router (OpenStreetMap, ODbL — the KML carries the attribution) and is cached in **`tools/route-cache.json`**, keyed by each leg's waypoint string. That cache is committed on purpose — the default build and `--check` never touch the network, so they stay deterministic and offline, and OSM shifting under us cannot silently rewrite a committed map. Move a stop and the key moves, the cache misses, and the build **names the days that fell back to chords** instead of shipping them quietly. OSRM returns a point every few metres, so geometry is decimated with Douglas-Peucker at ~40 m — 112 KB total against My Maps' 5 MB cap. **`drivePts()` decides what bends the line, and it is not every pin:** `opt` stops are branches (routing through Fagradalsfjall sent day 10 to Reykjanes and back; Seyðisfjörður sent day 5 over a pass it may never cross) and `offroute` stops are unreachable by car. Both stay pinned. It is what the real-map view points at: per-day `<Folder>`s with a placemark per stop, a `LineString` leg and the overnight base, styled in the same `--d1..--d11` spectrum read straight out of `:root`. **My Maps has no API**, so importing it is a manual step — and the imported map is a **snapshot, not a view**: regenerating `docs/iceland-2026.kml` leaves the live map unchanged, so any edit to a stop's `name`, `note` or coordinates silently drifts out of sync until someone re-imports by hand. That bit in Aug 2026 — stripping a booking reference from the page left it still readable in a Day 1 placemark on the public map — and the Oct 2026 generic rewrite changed nearly every note, so the live map needs a re-import after it. **Anything sensitive has to be removed in both places.** Per-placemark editing in My Maps is usually faster than a re-import, which costs the ten-layer dance again. The map is capped at **10 layers** and an 11-folder import **silently drops the last one** — that is not a risk, it happened: the first real import lost Sep 29. So the file emits **10 folders**, with the departure day riding inside day 10's, and the generator **throws** rather than warns if it ever regenerates past ten. The live map is `MYMAP_MID` in `index.html`. Three traps the script exists to contain: KML `<coordinates>` is **`lon,lat`** where this project stores **`[lat, lon]`** (reversed in exactly one helper, `coord()`); KML colour is **`aabbggrr`**, so `#3cc9e8` is `ffe8c93c`; and `note`/`shot` carry real `<b>` and literal `&amp;`, so descriptions go in `CDATA` and everything else through an XML escape — `esc()` from the itinerary generator escapes `|` for markdown and is the wrong tool. The one `DEPARTURE` stop without `ll` is skipped.
- **`docs/itinerary.md`** is generated — run `node tools/build-itinerary.mjs` after changing the itinerary, or `--check` to fail if it's stale. **`tools/build-itinerary.mjs`** evaluates the `DATA` section of `index.html` in a `node:vm` sandbox and renders the day table, base table, **departure section** and booking checklist straight from `TRIP`/`DEPARTURE`; the remaining prose sections are transcribed in the script's own template, so page copy and doc copy have to be edited together. It throws if `TRIP` or `DEPARTURE` comes back empty, rather than silently dropping a section. It keys off the `/* ==== DATA ==== */` and `/* ==== MAP ==== */` comment markers — renaming those breaks it loudly, by design. It's a docs tool, not a build step: `index.html` still ships as-is.

## History and constraints
Built in Claude.ai artifacts, where `localStorage`/`sessionStorage` are blocked — the booking tracker started in-memory out of necessity. Outside that sandbox the constraint is gone, and persistence, PWA/offline, light theme and live Kp all shipped (Aug 2026). A per-day `.ics` export shipped and was removed on request; **Plan B — a highland edition behind a plan switcher — was dropped** too (both Aug 2026). **Oct 2026, after the trip, it was made generic for sharing:** every booking reference and order number, every price paid, the party's size and ages, the travellers' nationality and the embassy cards that followed from it, the flight itinerary, the rental company and the countdown came out; computed daylight, the live tide strip, the moonset times and every sun angle in the photo lines were removed on request; and the public history was **squashed to a single commit**, so nothing before it is in this repo. A local `personal-archive-2026` branch on the original machine keeps the personal version and is never pushed.

## Conventions
- Stay single-file plus PWA shell and dependency-free unless there's a strong reason not to. **The one exception is the My Maps iframe**, and it is gated rather than trusted: nothing third-party loads until the user asks for it and the network is known to be up, so the default page still has zero external subresources.
- Design tokens in `:root`; `--d1..--d11` day spectrum is an information system (`--d11` is the departure card only — nothing on the map uses it), not decoration; `--ember` = bookable, teal = soak. All theme-sensitive colors are tokens — never reintroduce literals. Deliberate exceptions kept literal: the soak teal, chip inks, the aurora border gradient, and the low-alpha green/amber tints.
- **Legend labels describe marker rules; statline numbers are trip facts.** Amber = "timed booking" (`tag:"book"`), teal = "walk-in soak" (`tag:"soak"`). The hero's anchor-tour and soak-option counts are hand-maintained trip facts, not derived — the source comment above `TRIP.stats` records the current tally and what each number counts, so update it and the number together. Some soaks are paid, timed tickets and carry `book` (Blue Lagoon, Earth Lagoon); they count as soaks rather than tours, so they never inflate the anchor-tour number. Keep the two vocabularies disjoint — don't "reconcile" them into one count.
- **A day's `depart` is sometimes load-bearing, not taste.** Day 7 departs 07:55 because whale check-in closes 08:50 for the 09:30 sailing and the drive from Mývatn is 45 min; a missed check-in is charged in full. This time has moved every time the tours were rebooked (GG1 09:45 → GG2 09:30 → GG2 12:30 → GG2 09:30, with the Saltvík ride going 10:00 → 14:00 and 1.5 hrs → 1 hr). **The two bookings used to collide** — a 10:00 ride ending after an 11:50 check-in — and the current split, whales in the morning and horses at 14:00, is what pulled them apart; the stop order in the array follows it, so don't reshuffle day 7 without reading both stop notes. **Don't push it to 08:10 for the Dimmuborgir breakfast either** — service starts 08:00, the last departure that still makes check-in is 08:05 with zero margin, and a missed check-in is charged in full; the breakfast gets packed instead. Day 10 is the same shape: `08:10` exists because Into the Glacier starts 10:30 at Klaki, 1 hr 40 away up Road 550, and the day's stop list was cut down (Raufarhólshellir dropped, Hraunfossar demoted to an either/or) to give Fagradalsfjall real daylight — see the comment above the day. **Fagradalsfjall deliberately carries no clock in its `time`**: it is the alternative to Krauma, not a stop after it, so its `drive` is measured from Hraunfossar — the branch point — and the run-sheet must not be "fixed" to chain through it. (This branch used to point the other way, with Hraunfossar as the swap; it was inverted Aug 2026.) Don't "tidy" a departure time without checking what constrains it — the reason is in a comment above the day.
- Preserve accessibility floor: visible `:focus-visible` styles, keyboard-operable map stops (`Enter`/`Space`), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, print stylesheet working (all `<details>` auto-expand on `beforeprint`; controls, map links and the Kp strip hide in print).
- **Anything derivable from `TRIP` is derived, not typed into the markup** — `#st-km`, `#st-stops`, `#st-soak` and `#st-flex` are all written by `render()`. `buildReservations()` still handles a night with no stay, should one be removed. `10 nights` and the `$145–255` room-rate range are the only intentionally static numbers.
- **Cloudflare rewrites `mailto:` links** in HTML on the deployed site (Email Address Obfuscation), swapping them for a `/cdn-cgi/` path decoded by an injected script that is not in the service worker's `SHELL`, so an obfuscated address renders as garbage offline. No `mailto:` link is left in the markup today; if you add one, wrap it in `<!--email_off-->` / `<!--email_on-->`, Cloudflare's own opt-out. Emails inside the inline `<script>` (stop notes) are untouched because the rewriter only walks HTML.
- All asset paths stay **relative** so subpath hosting works. The sole exception is the `og:url` / `og:image` social-preview pair, which scrapers require to be absolute — update those two if the deploy host moves.
- CSS: flat selectors, tokens over literals; watch specificity collisions. Two live examples: the day-dimming rule needs `#icemap .route path.dim` because a bare `#icemap .dim` loses to `#icemap .route path`; and print token overrides must target `:root[data-theme="light"]`/`[data-theme="dark"]` too, or the theme attribute out-specifies them.
- UI copy: sentence case, plain verbs, field-log tone. Mono face for anything data-like.
- `localStorage` keys are namespaced `iceland26.*`.
- **No personal data — this plan is public and meant to be shared.** No booking references or order numbers, door codes, loyalty numbers, prices actually paid, party size or ages ("2 adults + 1 child", "our kid"), nationality or residency status, flight numbers or home airports, or anything a confirmation email said. Write for a reader: "book the 09:30 sailing ahead", not "booked, ref …"; "kids" or an operator's own age limit, not one family's child. Published operator facts — age and height limits, cancellation terms, opening hours, public phone numbers and emails — are fine and useful. **Confirmation numbers don't come back either**, and nothing may be "restored" from the archive branch.
- **Sky Lagoon is strictly 12+.** The plan is written to work with kids, so it appears only as that caveat on the Blue Lagoon stop, with Laugardalslaug as the family backup. Don't promote it to a stop.

## Backlog
- **Day km are the routed through-route distance, measured, not estimated.** Set from OSRM in Aug 2026 and rounded to the nearest 10; `stats.km` is their exact sum (2,250) and must stay so. Re-measure with `node tools/build-kml.mjs --route`, which prints declared against routed for every leg. **What they exclude is deliberate:** `opt` stops are branches, so the number is what you drive if you do the planned stops. The three optionals that cost real distance carry it in their own notes — Petra's **+68 km** (Route 1 cuts inland to Egilsstaðir; Petra's commits you to the fjord coast, and it is the biggest detour on the trip), Seyðisfjörður **+53 km** over Fjarðarheiði, and Fagradalsfjall **+85 km**, which is measured against the Krauma day because it *replaces* Krauma rather than adding to it. The old hand-authored figures were wrong in both directions — day 8 read `~390` and routes at 450, day 1 read `~100` and routes at 69 — and day 8 was separately described as 430 km in two food notes and the daylight comment, so three numbers disagreed about the longest drive of the trip.
- Persist the open/closed state of day cards across reloads.

## Deploy
Any static host; all asset paths are relative so subpath hosting works (currently GitHub Pages → `harshalgajare.com/Iceland/`). GitHub Pages: serve repo root. Azure Static Web Apps: app location `/`, no API, no build step.
