    The aurora game plan

    These dates straddle the autumn equinox — statistically one of the best northern-lights windows of the year — and 8 of your 10 nights are in dark-sky country. The routine:

    1. At dinner, check the aurora forecast and cloud-cover map at en.vedur.is. Gaps in cloud matter more than the Kp number — Kp 2–3 is already a show out here.
    2. Ask every hotel for a northern-lights wake-up call. Countryside hosts expect the request — make it at every check-in.
    3. Phone night mode, 3–10 seconds, propped on the car roof — it catches more color than your eyes do. A mini tripod earns its space in the bag.
    4. Push alerts: Hello Aurora (Icelandic) or My Aurora Forecast.

    Best dark skies → N2 · Seljalandsfoss N4 · Höfn / lagoon N6 · Mývatn N8 · Grundarfjörður — Kirkjufell under the lights is the trip photo

    The daily watch

    two checks a day, five minutes each

    Iceland weather is a wind problem, not a rain problem — and aurora hunting is a cloud problem, not a Kp problem. These are the sources locals actually use; every link opens in a new tab.

    Morning · weather & roads ~07:45 over breakfast

    en.vedur.is

    Icelandic Met Office — the national forecast, and the accurate one. Wind is in m/s: 15 m/s = hold the doors and expect a workout; 20+ m/s or an orange warning = rebuild the day around indoor stops; red = the car stays parked. Warning banners show right on the map.

    umferdin.is (road.is)

    Road Administration live map — closures, surface conditions, and measured gusts on the exact stretches you're driving. On the long days (4, 6, 8), check tomorrow's legs the night before too.

    safetravel.is

    ICE-SAR's alert feed — storm advisories, area and volcano closures. Register your travel plan once, and install the 112 Iceland app: one-tap emergency with your location, works everywhere.

    windy.com

    Visual gust animation — the supplementary check for timing photo stops and deciding which side of a headland to stand on.

    Evening · aurora ~19:00 at dinner

    vedur.is aurora forecast

    The one that matters: the Kp number plus the cloud-cover map. White gaps = clear sky — chase those. A clear-sky Kp 2–3 beats a cloudy Kp 6 every time at this latitude.

    Hello Aurora iOS / Android

    Icelandic-made push alerts plus live sightings from people actually standing outside in Iceland right now. Set the alert, phone by the bed, host knock requested as backup.

    NOAA SWPC 30-min forecast

    The US space-weather authority. The OVATION oval shows where the aurora is right now, refreshed every half hour — when the green blob drapes over Iceland, go outside.

    spaceweatherlive.com

    The nerd upgrade: live solar-wind speed and Bz. When Bz swings south (negative), a display usually follows within the hour.

    UAF Geophysical Institute

    University of Alaska's nightly + 27-day outlook — good for watching how your specific dates trend before you fly.

    Moon check: your dates wax toward a full moon ~Sep 25–26. Faint glows wash out; strong displays punch through. So lean on the alerts rather than patient sky-staring — and use the moonlit landscape as foreground.

    Field notes

    read once before wheels-down
    Volcano status — Reykjanes
    The eruption series is between episodes: the last one ended August 2025, magma is re-accumulating, and another eruption is considered likely at some point. Blue Lagoon and Grindavík are open. Past eruptions haven't touched KEF flights, Reykjavík, or the Ring Road — only a small zone near Grindavík. Book the lagoon flexible and check safetravel.is the week you fly.
    Weather & packing
    4–12 °C, wind always, rain somewhere on the loop. Waterproof shell + fleece layers, hats and gloves, waterproof shoes. Swimsuits and quick-dry towels ride in the day bag — you have four possible soaks (Blue Lagoon, Mývatn baths, Hofsós, GeoSea). Raincoats for everyone at the walk-behind waterfalls.
    Driving
    Hold car doors against the wind — the number-one rental damage claim in Iceland. Single-lane bridges in the southeast: first to arrive has right of way. Sheep own the road. 90 km/h max, headlights always on, zero-tolerance DUI. Check road.is each morning; no 4x4 needed on this route, but take gravel and sand/ash protection on the rental.
    Ocean safety — Reynisfjara & Djúpalónssandur
    Both beaches have genuinely dangerous sneaker waves. Obey the warning-light system at Reynisfjara, never turn your back on the sea, stay off the wet sand — and brief the 11-year-old hard: sneaker waves outrun sprinting adults.
    If a day goes sideways, trim these first
    Weather will steal at least half a day somewhere. On Plan B, the highland day carries its own built-in fallback (the classic coast day). Beyond that, protect the booked tours — Katla on Plan A, the whale watch on both — and cut in this order: the Siglufjörður loop (go straight down Route 1 — saves ~2.5 hrs), Stykkishólmur's backtrack, Raufarhólshellir or the Fagradalsfjall hike, then the 15-minute stops — Grjótagjá, Petra's Stones, GeoSea, Fontana.
    Food, fuel & phones
    N1/Orkan stations all along the route — bring a credit card with a PIN. Grocery stops (Bónus, Krónan, Nettó) for picnic lunches keep costs sane; restaurant meals run $25–45 a head. Langoustine night is Höfn (Pakkhús or Otto). eSIM from Nova or Síminn, or carrier roaming — Ring Road coverage is excellent.
